Delete your OjasFit account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open OjasFit and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account, your contact details and all your progress are deleted permanently, straight away.

If you cannot open the app

Email ojasfitindia@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the mobile number you signed in with. We will confirm it is you and delete everything within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything: name, mobile number, email, sign-in, profiles, workout history, weights and settings. We keep nothing afterwards.
