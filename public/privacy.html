Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect it.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Who we are

OjasFit is a home fitness app available at ojasfit.co.in and on Google Play. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

What the app stores, and where

To build your plan and show your progress, the app keeps the following only in storage on your own device:

  • the name or nickname you enter for each profile;
  • your goal, fitness level, training days and any physical limitations you select (for example knee pain);
  • height and weight, if you choose to enter them;
  • your workout history, personal-best times, weight log, water log, points and settings.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We do not run servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, phone number or email.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

Backups you create

If you use Profile → Download backup, the app creates a file on your device containing your data. Where you keep or send that file is up to you.

Services your phone provides

  • App delivery. The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may record technical details such as your IP address when the app is downloaded or updated. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Voice coaching. When the voice coach is on and no recorded voice is available, the app uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech, which may use your phone maker's or Google's speech service to read out exercise names. Only exercise names and coaching phrases are spoken, never your personal data. You can turn the voice off in Settings.
  • Sharing. If you tap a WhatsApp share button, the app opens WhatsApp with a message you can edit before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Erase everything, remove an individual profile, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because the data exists only on your device, this deletes it completely.

Children

OjasFit is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise any child using it.

Health information

OjasFit gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting if you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have not exercised for a long time.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing held by us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a grievance, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact us below and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if accounts or online backup are added, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect, and explain the change in the app.

Contact

Email: ojasfitindia@gmail.com

ऐप क्या सहेजता है, और कहाँ

आपका प्लान बनाने और प्रगति दिखाने के लिए ऐप ये जानकारी सिर्फ़ आपके अपने फ़ोन में रखता है: प्रोफ़ाइल का नाम, आपका लक्ष्य, फ़िटनेस स्तर, कसरत के दिन, चुनी गई शारीरिक तकलीफ़ें (जैसे घुटनों में दर्द), अगर आप डालें तो लंबाई और वज़न, और आपकी कसरतों का रिकॉर्ड, सर्वश्रेष्ठ समय, वज़न, पानी, अंक और सेटिंग।

यह जानकारी हमें या किसी और को नहीं भेजी जाती। हम इसे देख नहीं सकते।

हम क्या नहीं करते

हम अकाउंट, फ़ोन नंबर या ईमेल नहीं माँगते। हम विज्ञापन, एनालिटिक्स या ट्रैकिंग टूल इस्तेमाल नहीं करते। हम निजी जानकारी नहीं बेचते, न किराए पर देते हैं, न साझा करते हैं।

आपके फ़ोन की सेवाएँ

ऐप की फ़ाइलें GitHub Pages से आती हैं, जो किसी भी वेबसाइट की तरह तकनीकी जानकारी (जैसे IP पता) दर्ज कर सकता है। आवाज़ वाला कोच, जब रिकॉर्ड की गई आवाज़ उपलब्ध न हो, आपके फ़ोन की टेक्स्ट-टू-स्पीच सेवा से सिर्फ़ कसरतों के नाम बोलता है, आपकी निजी जानकारी नहीं; इसे सेटिंग में बंद कर सकते हैं। व्हाट्सऐप बटन दबाने पर संदेश तभी जाता है जब आप ख़ुद भेजें।

डेटा मिटाना

प्रोफ़ाइल → सब कुछ मिटाएँ दबाएँ, ऐप का स्टोरेज साफ़ करें, या ऐप हटा दें। डेटा सिर्फ़ आपके फ़ोन में होने के कारण यह पूरी तरह मिट जाता है।
