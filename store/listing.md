# Play Store listing

Copy each block into Play Console → **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**.
English is the default listing; add Hindi under **Manage translations → Add your own translation → Hindi (hi-IN)**.
Character limits are checked by `node scripts/check-listing.mjs`.

### Full description (max 4000)
```
OjasFit is your personal fitness coach at home. No gym, no equipment, no confusion: open the app, press Start, and follow along in Hindi or English.

YOUR COACH PLANS YOUR WEEK
Tell OjasFit your goal, your fitness level and how many days a week you can train. Your coach builds each week around that, makes every week a little harder, and listens to you. Say a workout was too easy or too hard and the next one changes.

10 JOURNEYS FOR EVERY GOAL
• Shuruaat — First Steps: never exercised? Start here
• Walk to Fit: a walking plan that grows week by week
• Fat se Fit: calorie-burning circuits
• Desi Taqat: akhada-style dand and baithak strength
• Surya Shakti: build up to 12 Surya Namaskars
• Shakti — Strength for Women: tone and strengthen at home
• Easy on Joints: low-impact strength, no jumping
• Chair Fit: seated and supported, the gentlest start
• Desk Detox: short daily fixes for a stiff back
• Shaadi Ready: 6 weeks before the big day

PEAKS: BEAT YOUR OWN RECORD
Eight named workouts done against the clock, from Nilgiri to Kanchenjunga. The work never changes, so a faster time means you really are fitter.

MADE FOR REAL INDIAN HOMES
• Short on time? One tap makes any session shorter
• Quiet mode removes all jumping, for flats and late nights
• Knee, back or shoulder trouble? Exercises are swapped automatically
• Works in a small room, in any clothes
• Works offline once installed

GUIDED, STEP BY STEP
• 52 exercises with animated demonstrations and clear instructions
• Timers, rest breaks and a voice coach in Hindi or English
• "Switch sides" and "halfway" cues so you never lose track

STAY MOTIVATED
• Streaks, points, ranks and badges
• Minutes-per-week chart and your best times
• Weight log with BMI using the cut-offs recommended for Indian adults
• Daily water tracker and practical tips on Indian food and habits
• Share your workout on WhatsApp

THE WHOLE FAMILY ON ONE PHONE
Up to 6 profiles, each with its own plan and progress.

FREE AND PRIVATE
OjasFit is free. There is no account to create, and everything you enter stays on your phone.

OjasFit gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have not exercised for a long time, check with your doctor before starting.
```
