### Full description (max 4000)
```
OjasFit is your personal bodyweight coach. No gym, no equipment: open the app, press Start, and train at home in Hindi or English.

A COACH THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
After every session you rate how hard it was and how clean your form was. The Coach uses that to set your next session: more reps when it felt light, less when it was too much, and an easier variation when form breaks down.

THREE FOCUSED JOURNEYS
• Bodyweight Shred: 8 weeks of high-energy intervals to burn fat
• Desi Strength: 8 weeks of calisthenics, dand and baithak to build muscle
• Living Room Express: 6 weeks of quiet 20-minute sessions for beginners and desk workers
Every journey moves through training phases: calibration, base, intensity, deload and a final peak week. It opens and closes with the same benchmark test, so you can see exactly how much fitter you got.

FOUR WAYS TO TRAIN
• Intervals: rounds with timed rest
• For time: race the clock
• AMRAP: as many rounds as you can before time runs out
• EMOM: a set every minute, rest with what is left

CLIMB THE SKILL LADDERS
Six ladders: push, shoulders, legs, hips and back, core and stamina. From wall push-ups to decline push-ups, from assisted squats to pistol squats. Every move has a clear goal; reach it with strict form in two workouts to unlock the next one.

PEAKS: OUR SIGNATURE WORKOUTS
Fifteen workouts named after India's mountains, from Nilgiri to Kanchenjunga, done against the clock. The recipe never changes, so a faster time is real progress. Climb each one three ways: Base Camp, Ridge or Summit, each with its own best time.

TATVA DRILLS
Five short drills named after the five elements. Agni for explosive power, Vayu for speed, Prithvi for leg strength, Jal for mobility and Akash for core control. Your coach adds one to every session.

FULL SESSIONS, YOUR LENGTH
Choose 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Every session runs warm-up, drill, main workout, accessory strength and cool-down.

ADAPT ANY SESSION TO YOUR DAY
• Quiet mode: no jumping, for flats and late nights
• Small space: stays within a yoga mat
• Short on time: cut any day to 20 or 15 minutes
• Sore today: skip knees, lower back, shoulders or wrists

GUIDED, STEP BY STEP
• 50 exercises with photo demonstrations and clear form cues
• Big rep counters and timers you can read mid-workout
• Voice coaching in Hindi or English

SHARE YOUR RESULT
Finish a session and share a result card to WhatsApp or Instagram in one tap.

FREE, PRIVATE AND BACKED UP
OjasFit is free. Sign in with your email, and your progress is saved to your account, safe even if you change phones. No ads and no tracking. Several people can keep separate profiles on one account.

OjasFit gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have not exercised for a long time, check with your doctor before starting.
```
